## 独立三选一升级弹窗

Use case: ui-mockup. Production art direction board for Chinese ink fantasy mobile roguelike 画灵. FOUR separate portrait screens side by side, each screen shows ONLY A BATTLE-PAUSED SKILL UPGRADE MODAL. ABSOLUTELY NO LEVEL SELECTOR, NO CHAPTER MAP, NO LEVEL NODES, NO START LEVEL BUTTON. Behind each modal dim an atmospheric hand-painted scene: autumn cinnabar valley, teal whale sea, indigo celestial palace, pale snowy sleeping giant. Every screen features a large calligraphy title 笔法悟道 and subtitle 三选一 · 强化本局笔法, then THREE SEPARATE HORIZONTAL UPGRADE CARDS stacked vertically, large brush skill illustration on left, title/level progression on right and concise benefit, four small progression diamonds at bottom. Card option names are 喷墨 Lv1→Lv2, 阔锋 Lv2→Lv3, 留墨 Lv1→Lv2. All options are brush upgrades, NOT animals, not standalone item rewards. Each chapter has a fully distinct material language: valley rolled ivory xuan paper and cinnabar seals with maple leaf corner; sea lustrous pearl and pale turquoise scalloped shell with curling wave edge; palace midnight-blue jade tablet, golden orbit rails and stars, hanging tassels; snow torn glacier white frost paper, angular ice edge, red thread stitch and subtle cairn rune. Bright readable cream paper valley and snow cards, light pearl sea cards, deep indigo gold palace cards. Exquisite large typography, hand drawn traditional Chinese brush illustrations, fine irregular gold/ink borders, subtle depth. Premium, restrained, atmospheric, clearly usable high fidelity game UI. Flat 2D screen compositions, no perspective, no phone hardware, no watermark or annotations. Landscape image four tall portrait screens.
